    Welcome, Vault Hunter

    SHiFT Redeemer finds SHiFT codes across the web and redeems them on your account automatically: Golden Keys, Diamond Keys, skins, the lot.

    • Finds codesCommunity feeds, official Steam news, Reddit, guides and deal sites.
    • Redeems them for youFor every game and platform you choose, never the same one twice.
    • Stays on your computerYour sign-in is only ever sent to SHiFT. Saved passwords go into Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain.

    Setup takes about two minutes. You can change anything later.

    Connect your SHiFT account

    Rewards go to the SHiFT account you sign in with, the same one you use at shift.gearboxsoftware.com.

    Link your gaming platforms

    SHiFT can only deliver rewards to platforms linked to your SHiFT account. Link every platform you play on. This happens on the SHiFT website, not in this app.

    Pick your games & platforms

    Most people leave everything on. Narrow it down if you only play some games.

    ⚠ Save your password without encryption?

    If you continue, your SHiFT password will be saved as readable text in . That means:

    • Anyone who can use this computer account can open the file and read it.
    • Any program running as you, including malware, can read it.
    • Backups or cloud-synced folders may copy it somewhere else.

    If you continue, make sure your SHiFT password isn't used for anything else. Or don't save it: you're still signed in now, and you'll just need to sign in again when SHiFT's session expires (auto-pilot waits until you do).
